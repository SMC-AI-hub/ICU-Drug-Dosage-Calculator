What is new in this build (Junior Clinical-UX Redesign)
------------------------------------------------------
1. Task-First Home Dashboard:
   - 3 primary workflow paths: 🚨 Emergency Now, 👶 Guided Patient Wizard, 💊 Find a Drug.
   - Quick patient presets (5y 18.3kg, 1y 9.6kg, Term newborn 3.4kg, 28+3 preterm 1.1kg).
   - Junior Doctor & Nurse Quick-Start Guide modal.

2. 3-Step Guided Patient Entry Wizard:
   - Step 1: Population selector (Neonate <28d vs Child/Adolescent 1m–18y).
   - Step 2: Measured Weight vs Gestational Age (weeks+days) + Fenton preterm curves / WHO medians.
   - Step 3: Optional Renal/Electrolyte labs (Creatinine for eGFR/CrCl, K+, Na+, Glucose).

3. Unified 9-Part Clinical Dose Card:
   - Clear visual hierarchy with prominent DOSE and VOLUME TO DRAW UP.
   - Verified check tags: [✓ Dose verified], [✓ Volume verified], [⚠ Estimated weight], [⚠ Ceiling applied].
   - Collapsible Calculation Formula & Working expander.
   - Sub-0.1 mL micro-volume dilution instructions.
   - ISMP High-Alert Independent Double-Check interactive checklist for high-risk drugs.
   - 1-tap "📋 Copy Dose Note" for clinical handovers and EMR notes.

4. Emergency Resuscitation Mode & CPR Timer:
   - Real-time CPR Cycle Timer with 2-minute rhythm check & adrenaline alert.
   - Weight-based Defibrillation energy table (1st shock 2 J/kg, 2nd shock 4 J/kg, Cardioversion 1 J/kg).
   - Emergency bolus medication quick-reference.

5. Neonatal Calculators Suite:
   - Feed volume progression calculator: 2-hourly (12/d), 3-hourly (8/d), and 4-hourly (6/d) aliquots.
   - Glucose Infusion Rate (GIR mg/kg/min) calculator.
   - Neonatal interval banding guidance for Gentamicin, Vancomycin, Caffeine, and Paracetamol.
